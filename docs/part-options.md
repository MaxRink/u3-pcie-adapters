# Part options, alternate card versions and purchase plan

Status 2026-11-13. Covers the x8 card (2 drives), the x16 3-drive card and the x16 4-drive card, rev F (drives on the solder side, see the
card README for the open question of moving them to the component side).
Nothing in this document has been ordered. Prices are single-source snapshots from the day above and move quickly;
re-check stock before ordering.

All board versions are produced by the same generator (`designs/x16-u3-card/gen`). Switches:
`U3_DRIVES=3|4` for the x16 card, `U3_CLK=cdcdb400` for the clock version. A switch only changes the clock buffer
area, the per-drive blocks and the PCIe lanes are generated by the same code.

### The CDCDB400 version in detail

Pin use (datasheet SNAS833B, table 4-1): 1 CKPWRGD_PD# tied to 3.3 V, 2 VDDR, 3/4 CLKIN, 5 SADR0 open,
6/7 SMBus tied to 3.3 V (bus not used), 8 to 11, 17 and 30 are NC and stay open, 12/16/21/25/29/31 VDD,
15 and 18 (OE0#, OE1#) tied high so the two unused outputs are off, 24 (OE2#) to GND, 26 (OE3#) open (internal
pull-down means enabled). Outputs CK2 (22/23) and CK3 (27/28) sit on the same pins as DIF2/DIF3 of the standard
part, so all clock routing after the buffer is unchanged. The input moves from pins 5/6 to 3/4, which shifts the
input pair by 1 mm at each buffer.

Things to know before choosing it:

* Output impedance is 85 ohm by default. 100 ohm needs one SMBus register write (OUTSET bit 5) after every power-up,
  and this layout ties the buffer's SMBus pins high. The REFCLK traces are 100 ohm. An 85 ohm source into a 100 ohm
  line gives roughly 8 % more swing at the drive and is common practice, but it is a deviation from the standard
  boards, where source and line match.
* It has no PLL, so every stage is a plain fan-out buffer. That removes the zero-delay stage and its PLL filter on the
  4-drive card, and with it the "drives C and D are Gen3 only" restriction on jitter grounds (additive jitter with the
  PCIe 4.0 filter is 63 fs per stage).
* Propagation delay is 0.5 to 3 ns per stage. Drives C and D on the 4-drive card sit behind three stages, so their worst
  case is 9 ns of buffer delay plus about 2.5 ns of trace against the 12 ns transport budget of a common-clock link.
  Typical parts are far inside that, the worst case leaves no margin for the host's own clock tree. The x8 and the
  3-drive card have at most two stages and are unaffected.
  The review therefore recommends the CDCDB400 version for the x8 and the 3-drive card only.
* Each repeater loses the dedicated 100 nF at VDDR (no room next to the shifted input vias). VDDR shares the 100 nF
  3.7 mm away on the 3.3 V ring.

### Planned leaded version (TPS16630)

Not laid out. The eFuse sits in a 5.2 mm box in the gap between two receptacles with its support parts in 1.35 mm
slots; an HTSSOP-20 (6.5 x 6.4 mm over the leads) needs that gap re-planned, and on the last drive it collides with the
rev F test pads. Circuit for when it is done (datasheet SLVSET9G):

* ILIM 4.7 k gives 3.83 A (I = 18 / R in k).
* UVLO/OVP divider from 12V_SEL: 100 k, 5.9 k, 9.1 k to GND. UVLO node between the first two (rising 9.2 V), OVP node
  between the last two (rising 15.1 V). Thresholds are 1.2 V.
* dVdT 10 nF gives about 2.5 ms ramp at 12 V. MODE to GND for auto-retry, open for latch-off.
* IMON 27.9 uA/A: 10 k gives 0.28 V/A (the standard boards read 0.25 V/A across 1 k).
* PGOOD is active high like the TPS25982's PG, so the reset gating stays as it is. The output type (open drain or
  push-pull) could not be confirmed from the text I had and must be checked before layout.
* On-resistance is 31 mohm against 2.7 mohm: 0.28 W and 93 mV drop at 3 A per drive. Acceptable, but the copper under
  the PowerPAD has to carry that heat.

| Amphenol PSAS4F2130201TR | planned version | Digi-Key 271 pcs, EUR 7.36 at 1, 6.25 at 10, 5.86 at 25 |

Sixteen pieces cover five x8 cards (10) or five 3-drive cards (15), not five 4-drive cards (20).

The 201TR differs in the board retention: 1.45 mm NPTH pegs and plated 0.80 mm hold-downs in 2.60 mm pads on a
39.36 mm span (drawing psas4f213020xxx rev 1, numbers second-hand). The drawing itself could not be downloaded
(the Amphenol CDN refuses automated requests), and the footprint is not built from three numbers. To finish this
version someone has to download the drawing by hand from the part page; then it is a new footprint in
`lib/u3adapter.pretty`, a check of the peg and hold-down positions against the lanes that pass under the housing, and
one routing run per card.

## 5. Assembly options

**A. Assembled by JLCPCB (recommended).** Both sides placed by the fab. Everything except the parts below is in the
LCSC catalogue under the numbers in the BOM files. Consign or hand-fit: the clock buffers (PI6CB33401 and CDCDB400
are not at LCSC), and the receptacles and eFuses if LCSC stock has moved.

**B. Fab-assembled passives, hand-fitted specials.** Let the fab place everything it stocks and fit these yourself:

| Part | Package | How |
|---|---|---|
| U.3 receptacles (bottom side) | SMD right angle, 0.8 mm pitch, pegs | iron, drag soldering, flux |
| eFuse | VQFN-24 with exposed pad | hot air or hot plate, paste on the pad |
| Clock buffers | VQFN-32 with exposed pad | hot air or hot plate, paste on the pad |
| J5, fan header J7, I2C header J6, 12 V jumpers | through-hole and SMD headers | iron |

Everything added in rev F (features 1 to 6) is 0603 or larger, or leaded, and on the top side, for this reason.

**C. Fully hand-soldered.** Possible on the standard boards with hot air, not with an iron alone: each drive block
has about 35 parts in 0402 and SC-70 and the two QFNs. Per card that is 107 (x8), about 150 (3-drive) or 193
(4-drive) placements, 13 to 36 of them on the bottom side (the receptacles are bottom-side by design, so no version
of these cards is truly single-sided).

**D. An iron-only board (0603 and leaded parts throughout).** Planned, not designed. It needs the TPS16630 version
from section 3, a leaded clock buffer does not exist in a PCIe Gen4-capable part, and the sideband block does not have
room for 0603 parts between the lanes on six layers. It would mean a longer strip or eight layers and a full re-route
of the per-drive block. Given option B covers the same need, this is not recommended.

### Order of events

1. Decide the card type and whether the 16 LCSC receptacles are enough. For the 4-drive card, or more than five cards,
   either the 201TR version has to be finished first or 270 pieces of the 021TR bought.
2. Order boards and assembly from the `fab/` folder of the chosen version with the matching BOM file.
3. Order the specials from Mouser in one basket (eFuse, clock buffer) and the receptacles from LCSC with the board
   order.
4. Before the first drive goes in: blank-board fit test of the window edge (README), then the bring-up list in
   `docs/reviews/`.
