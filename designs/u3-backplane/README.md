# Cabled U.3 backplane

A small NVMe backplane in the style of a Supermicro CSE-SAS-733TQ: U.3 drives stand on vertical receptacles,
every cable leaves sideways on the underside, and a host card in a PCIe x16 slot feeds it over SlimSAS 8i cables.
It is meant to sit flat in the top compartment of a Jonsbo N5 behind the two front 120 mm fans, with the drives
loaded from the top (so they can be swapped with the lid off), inside a printed cage.

| Board | Size | Drives | Cables | Files |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Backplane, 4 drives | 79.6 x 90.5 mm | 4 | 2 x SlimSAS 8i | `boards/backplane`, `fab/backplane` |
| Backplane, 6 drives | 119.6 x 90.5 mm (one 120 mm fan wide) | 6 | 3 x SlimSAS 8i | `boards/backplane6`, `fab/backplane6` |
| Host card, passive | 131 x 44 mm, PCIe x16 | 4 per card | 2 x SlimSAS 8i | `boards/host_passive`, `fab/host_passive` |

Two 6-drive modules side by side give 12 drives across both fans. That is 48 lanes: six 8i cables and three
x16 slots set to x4x4x4x4 (or a PCIe switch card).

All boards use the project's 6-layer rule set (JLC06161H-3313, 85 ohm pairs 0.157 / 0.152 mm, see
`design-rules/`). They are generated by the scripts in `gen/`; nothing is drawn by hand.

## Mechanics

Drawings: `docs/backplane_mech.svg`, `docs/backplane6_mech.svg`. Board-only STEP models are in `fab/*/`.

- Frame: seen from the drive side, origin top-left, x right, y down.
- Drive receptacles (Amphenol PSAS5F3130021TR, vertical, 8.15 mm tall) at x = 9.8 + 20 * i, y = 48.5, long axis
  along y, 20 mm pitch. Module pitch is 80 / 120 mm with 0.4 mm between boards.
- M3 holes: (3.2, 15.5), (W - 3.2, 15.5) and (36 + 40 * c, 86.3) for every drive pair c.
- Underside (all right angle, tallest part 13 mm, the cage keeps 15 mm clear):
  - y = 90.5 edge: one SlimSAS 8i per drive pair, centred at x = 15.8 + 40 * c.
  - y = 0 edge, left to right from the drive side: fan control in (1x4), 12 V in, 12 V out (Mini-Fit Jr 2x4),
    fan + fan control out (1x8).
- Slots between the drives let air through when the board stands upright. Lying flat they are not needed.

Assumed, not measured: the N5 top compartment has room for the cage (Max: "plenty of space"), and the drive's end
face sits about 6.5 mm above the board. The Amphenol drawing gives the 8.15 mm body height only.

## Electrical design

**Lanes.** One x4 link per drive, no lane crossings and exactly one via per net. Host transmit (PET) runs on L1
and drops to L6 behind the SlimSAS pads, drive transmit (PER) runs on L6. Lanes are 19 to 43 mm long. Pairs are
matched by the position of their vias, without serpentines (largest skew 0.004 mm, see
`fab/*/*-lengths.json`). 220 nF AC caps sit on the drive's lane 0 and 1 transmit pairs only
(SFF-TA-1001), S15 (HPT0) is grounded.

| SlimSAS connector | Port A (pins 1-18, host lanes 0-3) | Port B (pins 19-37, host lanes 4-7) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| first (left) | drive 1 | drive 0 |
| second | drive 3 | drive 2 |
| third (6-drive board) | drive 5 | drive 4 |

Sidebands follow SFF-9402: every port carries its own REFCLK, PERST#, CPRSNT#, CWAKE# and BP_TYPE (pulled low).
Cables must be the crossover type the spec describes (row A at one end is row B at the other).

**Power and hot plug.** 12 V enters on a PCIe 8-pin (Mini-Fit Jr) and is passed on to a second one for the next
module. Each drive has its own TPS259824 eFuse: about 3.9 A limit, soft start (10 nF on dV/dt), and it is only
switched on while a drive is present (PRSNT# or IfDet# low through a 74LVC1G00). A TPS3808 watches the drive's
12 V and releases the drive's PERST# about 125 ms after the rail is good, so a freshly inserted drive always sees
a proper reset. An empty bay is unpowered.

Daisy chain limit: a PCIe 8-pin is specified for 150 W. Four 25 W drives are 100 W, six are 150 W, so chain two
4-drive modules only with drives that stay under about 18 W each, and give every 6-drive module its own lead.

Host side of hot plug: the backplane is electrically safe to plug and pull. Whether the OS notices on its own
depends on the root port. A bifurcated consumer slot normally has no hot-plug slot capability, so use
`echo 1 > /sys/bus/pci/devices/<bdf>/remove` before pulling and `echo 1 > /sys/bus/pci/rescan` after inserting.
Boards that expose "PCIe hot plug" or Intel VMD for the slot handle it without that.

**Management.** One TCA9548A SMBus switch (address 0x71) gives every drive its own channel (channel = drive
number). One TCA9554 per drive pair (0x20, 0x21, 0x22) reads PRSNT#, IfDet#, IfDet2# and drives PWRDIS for both
drives (bits 0-3 even drive, 4-7 odd drive). The upstream bus is the 2-wire pair of port A on the first SlimSAS
connector; that port's PERST# also resets the switch. 3.3 V comes from an on-board LDO fed from 12 V (drive side,
above the fan header). L4 carries 12 V above the drives and to every eFuse and 3.3 V everywhere else.

**Fan.** The 1x8 header's pins 1-4 are a standard 4-pin fan header (GND, 12 V through a 1.1 A PTC, tach, PWM).
PWM and tach come from the "control in" header (mainboard fan header or the previous module), and PWM is passed
on by pins 5-8 to the next module. The 12 V pins of the control headers are not connected, so nothing is fed back
into the mainboard.

## Host card (passive)

PCIe x16 edge to two SlimSAS 8i, for a slot set to x4x4x4x4. The connectors sit on the underside at the top edge,
which keeps the lane order of the fingers. PET has one via, PER two. A 9DBL0442 buffer (bypass mode) gives each
port its own REFCLK, a TCA9546A (0x77) gives each port its own SMBus channel (channel 0 reaches the backplane's
switch), a TCA9554 (0x27) reads CPRSNT# and BP_TYPE of the four ports, PERST# goes to every port through 33 ohm
and the four CWAKE# lines join WAKE#. Trace length is 23 to 30 mm per lane.

A passive card plus a 0.5 m cable plus the backplane (19 to 43 mm) is past what PCIe 4.0 allows without
re-equalisation. Expect Gen3 to work and Gen4 to depend on the board and the cable. For Gen4, use a redriver or
retimer card.

**Redriver and retimer versions: not built (decided 2026-11-13).** For PCIe 4.0 a retimer card is bought instead
(see below). A redriver card (4 x DS160PR810) was analysed but not laid out: it does not re-time, so Gen4 over
0.5 m would stay uncertain. A retimer card cannot be laid out from public data, TI's public DS160PT801 datasheet
has no ball map.

## What to buy

Cables (one per drive pair): SlimSAS SFF-8654 8i to 8i, straight, 85 ohm, "PCIe 4.0", with sidebands, 0.5 m or
shorter.

| Cable | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| 3M 8ES8-1DF21-0.50 | reference-grade twinax, 85 ohm; the safest choice |
| 10Gtek CAB-8654/8654-8i-P-85 | "PCIe 4.0, 85 ohm, with sideband", 0.5 to 1 m, listed at about 15 USD (2026-11-13) |
| Delock 82303 (0.5 m) | SlimSAS 8i to 8i; impedance and sidebands not confirmed from the product page |

Avoid 100 ohm "SAS" cables and cables without sidebands: the drives would get no clock or reset.

Host card to buy instead of building one: a x16 to dual SlimSAS 8i **retimer** card, for example LR-LINK
LRNV9F24 or the C-Payne "SlimSAS PCIe gen4 host adapter x16" in its retimer version. Prices could not be read
from the vendor pages (LR-LINK sells on inquiry). Check before ordering that the card follows SFF-9402 on the
cable side (REFCLK and PERST# on every port); a card that only passes lanes will not bring up the drives.

## Check before ordering

- Pin 1-3 = 12 V on the Mini-Fit Jr footprints was taken from the Molex numbering; check with a PSU cable.
- MCP1703A SOT-223 pinout (VIN, GND, VOUT) and the TPS259824 land pattern split are from memory or inferred.
- eFuse values (390 ohm ILIM, 2.2 nF ITIMER, 10 nF dV/dt) come from the datasheet formulas as remembered.
- LCSC numbers are filled in only where known; empty fields in the BOM need a part chosen.
- The 9DBL0442 and the eFuse are QFNs. Everything else is leaded or 0402 and larger; hot air works for all of it.
- The U.3 receptacle's seated drive height and the connector offset on the drive were not available.

## Regenerating

```
cd designs/u3-backplane/gen
ND=4 python3 bp.py        # 4-drive backplane (ND=6 for six), about 30 min
python3 host.py           # passive host card
cd .. && tools/outputs.sh backplane u3_backplane "Cabled U.3 backplane, 4 drives"
```

`tools/outputs.sh` runs the DRC (`tools/drc.py`) and writes the Gerber zip, BOM, CPL, the board-only STEP, the lane
length report and the mechanical drawing.

`STAGE=place` skips the low-speed router and gives a placement DRC in seconds. KiCad 7.0.11. A full run takes 20 to 30
minutes per board.
